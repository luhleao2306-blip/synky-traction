# Landing photography

`public/traction-cover-motion.mp4`, `public/traction-cover-motion-mobile.mp4`, and the desktop/mobile poster images: a silent 10-second illustrative cover sequence assembled from two photorealistic scenes created with AI for Synky Traction. The scenes show fictional workplace conversations; they do not depict Synky staff, customers, or actual HR decisions. The video uses subtle camera motion and dissolves, with no audio. The poster frames also provide a motion fallback when autoplay is blocked.
